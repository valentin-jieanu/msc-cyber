# Unit 4: Software engineering and development methodologies (seminar slides)

Module: Launch into Computing · Formative activity (not graded) · Presented on 19 November 2026

## Task

The tutor asked each student to prepare a few slides on the two discussion questions in the section "Introduction to Software Engineering Principles", and to present them at the live seminar "Software Engineering and Development Methodologies":

1. Why do we need structured development models in software engineering?
2. How does software engineering balance innovation with reliability?

## Contents

- Why structured models: clear goals, finding mistakes early, and safety-critical industries.
- Waterfall, Agile and DevOps: when to use each one, and the main risk of each.
- How to balance innovation with reliability: small releases, automated tests, fast rollback and planning for problems.
- Case study: the CrowdStrike outage of July 2024.
